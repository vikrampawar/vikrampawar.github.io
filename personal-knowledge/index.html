Personal knowledge

Personal knowledge is a private tool used only by Vikram Pawar to search his own Gmail and Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • The app asks for read-only access to Gmail and Google Drive. It cannot send, change or delete anything.
  • Data is read only from the owner's own accounts and stored only in the owner's private storage.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
